WORDS

Whole-word recognition drill — stop hearing letters, start hearing language.

Plays common CW words and abbreviations as single rhythm shapes (TNX, FB, OM, 73, RST, QTH, DE, CQ...). 4 word options appear; tap the right one within 1.5 seconds.

The bridge from "Morse student" to fluent operator. Coming in Phase C.

SEND

Paddle sending practice — motor memory is the layer most apps skip.

Two big DIT/DAH buttons for mobile thumbs, configurable arrow keys for desktop, optional iambic mode. App scores your timing accuracy against ideal Morse rhythm.

Coming in Phase D — see CW-TRAINER-SPEC.md.
